# Product Deals

Product Deals allows you to showcase deals for your customers

![Enable product deals](image/product-deals.png)

## Enable Product Deals

![Enable product deals](image/wc--product-deals.png)


## Set Up Product Deals

In the **Product > General**, you need to enter regular price, sale price, sale price dates and sale quantity.

![Edit product deals](image/wc-edit_product_deals.jpg)


## Set Up Product Deal Of The Day

The product deal of the day should have sale price dates to is today. It means today is 2019-06-26, the product should have sale price dates to is 2019-06-26

![Edit product deals](image/wc-product-deal-of-the-day.png)
