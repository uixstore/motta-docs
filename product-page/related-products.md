### Options

![](image/related-products-optionss.png#alignright)

- **Related Products** - turn on this option to show related products in the single product page
- **Custom Related Products** - Display custom related products options for individual products (by categories, tags, or products)
- **Related Products Title** - enter related products title
- **Related Products By Categories** - enable/disable get related products by categories
- **Related Products By Parent Category** - enable/disable get related products by parent category
- **Related Products By Tags** - enable/disable get related products by tags
- **Related Products Numbers** - set the numbers of the related products you want to show
