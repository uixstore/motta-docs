# Product Tabs

Gives you complete control over your product page tabs.

## Enable Product Tabs

Just go to **WooCommerce > Settings > Products > Product Tabs** to enable this feature

![Enable product tabs](_images/wc-product-tabs.png)


## Change Default Product Tabs

After enabled the size guide from WooCommerce settings, you will see a new menu **Products > Product Tabs**. On this page,
you can re-order, or rename the core Description, Additional Information, and Reviews tabs

![Default product tabs](_images/wc-product-tabs-default.png)


## Add a new Product Tab

After enabled the size guide from WooCommerce settings, you will see a new menu **Products > Product Tabs**. On this page, just click the **Add New** button on top to add a new product tab for your products.

In the product tab editor, you can enter the product tab title, the content, the tab type, the categories


#### Global tabs

![Add Global tabs](_images/wc-global-tabs.png#alignright)


Create tabs that can be added to all products or special products via product categories.

- **Tab Title**  – add the product tab title
- **Tab Content**  – add the product tab content
- **Tab Type**  – select global type
- **Categories**  – show the tab on products of select categories. If you don't select any categories, the tab will show on all products

![Add Global tabs](_images/wc-tabs-product-frontend.png)


## Reorder Product Tabs
![Change product size guide](_images/wc--product-tabs-order.png#alignright)

Go to **Products > Product Tabs** to drag and drop product tabs
