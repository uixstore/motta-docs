## Active Products Filter

![](_images/product-filters-widget.png#alignright)

- Go to **Appearance > Widgets** add the Razzi - Products Filter widget to the Catalog Sidebar, the Catalog Filter Sidebar, the Catalog Filters Mobile.

- The Razzi - Products Filter widget works with the catalog page only.


## Products Filter Options

![](_images/products-filter-options.png#alignright)

- **Use ajax for filtering** - turn on AJAX for filtering helps you avoid reloading the whole page when filtering
- **Filtering products instantly (no buttons required)** - turn on this option for filtering products instantly without clicking a filter button.
- **Filter buttons position** - select a filter buttons possition
	- **Bottom of form** - Filter buttons are in the bottom of the form
	- **Bottom of filter items** - Filter buttons are in the bottom of the filter items
- **Update URL** - update URL to the browser without reloading the page
