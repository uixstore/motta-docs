### Filter by Price - Slider

![](_images/filter-by-price-slider.png)

![](_images/filter-by-price-ranges-options.png#alignright)

 - **Filter By** - select Price
 - **Display Type** - select Ranges
 - **Ranges** - each range on a line, separate by the - symbol. Do not include the currency symbol. Example:
 	- 10-19
	- 50-99
	- 100-199
	- 200 +
