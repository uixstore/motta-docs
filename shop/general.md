This section describes the general settings of a Catalog page (the Shop page).

![](image\customize_catalog_demo.png)

And this panel contains sections which have options that helping you control catalog page, just navigate to **Appearance > Customize > Catalog Page**. 

We will have a separated tutorial for controling it bellow. At this writing, we only introduce to you the general purposes of sections in this panel.

<img src="image\customize_catalog.png" data-origin="image\customize_catalog.png" alt="">

## Catalog General

![](image\customize_catalog_general.png)

1. **Catalog Layout**
    
    There are three styles

2. **Catalog View**

	There are three views: grid, list and extended

3. **Full width**

	Select this option to extend width of that page

4. **Show excerpt**

5. **Sale percentage**

	Turn on/off the sale percen 

6. **Product attribute**

7. **Other option**
