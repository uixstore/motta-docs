### Account Options

![](_images\header-account-options.png#alignright)

- **Account Icon Behaviour** :
	- **Open the account panel** - When you click the account icon, the account panel will open.
	- **Open the account dropdown** - When you hover on the account icon, the account dropdown will open.

- **Account Links** - To enable or disable items, or to reorder the items you want to show on account panel/dropdown
