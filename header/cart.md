### Cart Options

![](_images\header-cart-options.png#alignright)

- **Cart Icon Behaviour**
	- **Open the cart panel** - When you click the cart icon, the cart panel will open.
	- **Open the cart page** - When you click the cart icon, the cart page will open.

- **Counter Background Color** - Change the background color of counter cart
- **Counter Color** - Change the text color of counter cart
